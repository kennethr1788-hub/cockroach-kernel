## Structural boundary

This campaign does not reopen, amend, or add evidence to Gate 6 and is not
Gate 7. Gate 6 remains immutable. Gate 7 remains the held-out failure and
bounded AWS/CockroachDB campaign already defined by its own artifacts. A later
fresh-context black-box evaluation is a separate supplemental campaign.

## Stated goal

Test whether the exact frozen Gate 6 product candidate preserves its measured
recovery and safety behavior as controlled synthetic repositories increase in
file count and byte volume, without tuning the product or success rules after
results are observed.

## Stated outcome

The only successful outcome is a canonical 108-execution evidence set over the
same six state/loss classes and the same three fairly qualified methods across
three repository profiles, with all product trials restoring the exact expected
manifest, continuing executably, producing no unsafe acceptance, and leaving
zero trial residue. Every failure is retained. The evidence remains explicitly
synthetic, team-authored, private, non-production-scale, and unsuitable for
population inference.

## Frozen matrix

| Profile | Files | Generated bytes |
|---|---:|---:|
| small | 16 | 131,072 |
| medium | 64 | 4,194,304 |
| large | 128 | 67,108,864 |

Each profile crosses:

- six state/loss classes: committed-only, committed-plus-uncommitted,
  complete-loss, partial-loss, conflicting-stale, and clean-control;
- three methods: ordinary Git, Git plus Restic 0.19.0, and the frozen product;
- two repetitions with deterministic post-freeze seeds;
- rotated within-pair execution ordering.

The 128-file ceiling is deliberate. It tests meaningful byte scale while
keeping the product's canonical candidate record under the existing 64-KiB
record limit. The compact SHAKE256 generator prevents the payload from carrying
large test blobs and produces high-entropy bytes that do not collapse into a
trivial compression benchmark.

- exact source/event/loss pairing within every method comparison;
- 108/108 cleanup and zero residue;
- zero unsafe acceptance;
- product exact-manifest result 12/12 per size profile;
- product executable continuation 12/12 per size profile;

- unprivileged Linux x86_64 execution under inherited seccomp network denial;
- scanner-clean allowlisted payload with no credentials or private paths;
- one successful worker only, with every failed creation/readiness attempt
  deleted and reconciled before the next attempt;
- complete retrieval, local hash verification, exact-ID deletion proof, empty
  campaign inventory, and independent final review.

## Kill line

Stop immediately on source/hash drift, payload contamination, unknown or
unbounded price, spend-envelope breach, wrong worker shape/image/storage,
inability to delete an attempt, upload to an unverified worker, isolation or
network-denial failure, missing receipt, nondeterminism, false acceptance,
residue, evidence mismatch, or lifecycle-guard failure. No replacement is
allowed after payload upload or measured execution begins.
