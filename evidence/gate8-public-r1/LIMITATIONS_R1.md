# Gate 8 Evidence Limitations R1

- The real-workflow trace has one operator. It is not a public-user study or a
  population-wide usability result.
- The 18-session fresh-context campaign used stateless local model actors and
  six project-authored synthetic classes. It is machine evidence, not human
  evidence.
- The 84-case campaign used newly generated hidden synthetic inputs, but the
  generator and product implementation share project provenance. It is not
  statistically independent evidence.
- The 46,000-row CockroachDB workload and one-hour cloud path are bounded,
  single-region tests. They do not prove unlimited scale or production
  longevity.
- Gate 7 Runs 3, 4, and 5 failed and remain immutable evidence. Only Run 6
  completed every conjunctive Gate 7 requirement.
- The AWS post-exchange margin check passed at 901 seconds against a 900-second
  minimum. It is evidence for that frozen gate, not a general availability
  claim.
- Mathematical RunPod cost is not an exact provider billing receipt.
- The product reconstructs only captured, declared representations. It cannot
  recover arbitrary uncaptured bytes from nothing.
- No first-ever, only, perfect, safest, universal, or global-optimality claim is
  supported.
