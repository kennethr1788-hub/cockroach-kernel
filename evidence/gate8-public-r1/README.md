# Gate 8 Sanitized Public Evidence Subset R1

This directory is the compact, sanitized evidence surface for product
candidate `1c483b1930e629c9ecb6d73418b9554897dc08ad`.

- `SCORECARD_R1.md` maps direct evidence to the five official judging criteria.
- `CLAIM_TO_EVIDENCE_MANIFEST_R1.json` binds every public clause to a metric,
  test, repository-relative source receipt, SHA-256, evidence mode, and
  limitation.
- `ARCHITECTURE_BOUNDARY_R1.md` shows the AWS, CockroachDB, agent, advisory,
  and deterministic-authority boundaries.
- `LIMITATIONS_R1.md` prevents synthetic, bounded, or single-operator evidence
  from being presented as production or public-user proof.

Hidden inputs, raw oracle material, credentials, endpoints, account details,
and local private evidence are intentionally excluded. Their deterministic
local archive is separately hash-bound and is not authorized for public
release.
